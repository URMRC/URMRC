URMRC : Upper Rhine Mobile Robotic Challenge

The Upper Rhine Mobile Robotic Challenge (URMRC) is a robotics competition prepared, organized, and conducted as part of the activities of the RobotHub Académie project. It takes place during the BE 5.0 Industries du Futur trade fair and conference. The URMRC is a cross-border competition bringing together teams from France, Germany, and Switzerland. During its first two editions, the competition welcomed eight teams each year, composed of Bachelor's and Master's students from several higher education institutions across the Upper Rhine region.

Participants are given two months to develop perception, navigation, and control algorithms for a TurtleBot mobile robot. To ensure fairness among all teams and to focus the evaluation on software and algorithmic performance, the organizers provide each team with an identical TurtleBot platform.

The challenge consists of several missions, including trajectory tracking, obstacle avoidance, navigation in a confined environment, and autonomous parking maneuvers. Teams are evaluated on the robustness, accuracy, and overall performance of their algorithmic solutions under conditions representative of real-world mobile robotics applications.

Key dates:

  • Competition announcement: October 1, 2025

Overview

Teams develop advanced algorithms to tackle a series of challenges in mobile robotics using the TurtleBot robot. The competition missions include line following and obstacle avoidance in both an open area and a tunnel. In the second edition of the competition, the parking mission was replaced by a palletizing mission. Finally, the last mission consists of completing all the previous missions consecutively as part of a single run. You can consult the regulations for the first and second editions of the competition for more details.

MISSIONS
1

Line Following

The robot follows a straight and curved track and stops when it detects a black-and-white checkerboard.

2

Obstacle Avoidance – Open Area

The robot avoids randomly placed obstacles within a marked area and stops before a barrier.

03

Obstacle Avoidance – Closed Area

The robot crosses the area while avoiding obstacles. Camera use is prohibited inside.

04

Parking

The robot detects the parking area, identifies an available space, and parks correctly.

Storage/Retrieval Operation

The robot detects the occupied storage area, picks up the pallet, moves it from zone ① to zone ② without crossing other zones, and releases it correctly at the center.

05

Fifth Mission

Execute all missions in sequence.
